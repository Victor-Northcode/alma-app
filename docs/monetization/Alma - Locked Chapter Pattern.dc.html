C7The rule sheet — hand this to the developer
Free = natal chapter I only
«Core» is written in full, no blur, badge «free». Every other chapter of every system is locked — including chapter I of numerology, birth card, transits, solar, astrocartography, synthesis and pairs.
The locked chapter has six parts, always in this order
1 numeral «2 / 16» · 2 overline + title · 3 «read from» with real positions · 4 one genuinely written paragraph (~40 words, from the engine) · 5 blurred unwritten text — blur 4.5 px, opacity .5, gradient fading into the page, never selectable · 6 ✦ + one line of what follows + one price button sitting on top of the blur.
The button stands on the blur, not below it
The blurred column runs behind the ✦, the line and the CTA to the bottom of the screen — the reader sees text continuing under the button, which is what makes the purchase feel like opening rather than buying. The blur must never end above the button leaving a clean gap.
The blurred text is filler, not content
The engine writes only the opening paragraph before payment — no tokens are spent on the rest. The blur is 3 paragraphs of neutral filler from the client, unreadable by design, never copyable, never in the accessibility tree (semantics: «locked text»).
The price depends on the system, nothing else
Static readings (natal, numerology, birth card, astrocartography, synthesis) → «Unlock and read · $4.99 · yours forever». Living ones (transits, solar return, the daily horoscope, Alma's questions) → «All of Alma · $9.99 / month» + renewal line. Pairs → $4.99 per person, consumable, unlimited people.
Deleted screens
The black «Unlock to read» wall and the «Alma has written a lot for you today / See the plans» cap screen. A locked chapter is never a separate route — it is the chapter itself, half-written.
Compatibility is never a dead card
Tap → add a person (no price on that screen) → their chart is calculated free → the «Attraction» chapter opens in this same pattern, teaser written, rest blurred, $4.99. A subscriber with an unused monthly check sees «included in your month» instead of the price.
