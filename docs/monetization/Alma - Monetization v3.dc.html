Weekly $4.99 and yearly $78.99 are off the shelf. One subscription, $9.99/mo. P1–P3 of the old Plans canvas are dead; V8 replaces them.
The archive top-up is gone
$38.99 archive, $29.99 bump and the $33.00 upgrade disappear with old P4/P5. The bundle is a flat $19.99 for five readings; no credit maths on any screen.
Doors are $4.99, and there are five

The pill loses its job
«All of Alma» floating over Today is a proactive offer on every screen — против принципа «≤1 за сессию». Recommendation: drop the pill, sell the living layer from the locked blocks it already sits next to (V6). Needs your call.
