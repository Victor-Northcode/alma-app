The cabinet, the reading, the money, the arrival

Rebuilt 1:1 from mobile/flutter/alma — palette, type scale, metrics and copy are the real values from the code. Reference screens by badge (S1…S52) to iterate. 52 screens: the four tabs, all eight systems inside, the reading, the money, the selling layer with the pill's full lifecycle, pushes, sign-in, every waiting state and the whole arrival — with the app's motion vocabulary applied.

UI/UX — why it sells without pushing
One accent per screen. One gold button max; alternatives are outlines, quiet actions are veils — hierarchy by height (56/54/50), not by shouting.
The invitation is an event, not furniture. The pill waits 6 s (the screen is read first), lives 11 s, nods twice, evaporates; ≤3 appearances, 90 s of silence between. Never for subscribers, never over the parchment.
Facts before price. The sheet and the paywall open with three checkable facts, auto-renewal is disclosed above the button, "Not now" and "Restore" are always reachable, and "every calculation stays free" is said on every selling surface.
Walls speak human. Every dead end names the reason and gives exactly one action: add a person, enter birth time, see the plans, try again.
State is never lost. All four tabs live for the whole session (the conversation survives tab switches), drafts stay in the box on failure, reading position survives the bar, and profile changes reload data — not the screen.
Motion vocabulary — "the sky moves, the interface doesn't"
Controls are fast: tap 120 ms (a settle, never a scale), state change 240 ms, page 300 ms, sheet 380 ms, chapter turn 420 ms, purchase reveal 620 ms — the slowest thing that isn't sky.
Arrival cascade: header first, each block +70 ms, 16 pt rise — once per visit, never replayed on data refresh.
Settled art breathes: the moon medallion, Alma's light, the ceremony drawing — slow scale 1→1.04 on their own clocks, so a finished drawing never reads as frozen.
Self-drawing: every system's art draws itself on first sight (rings sweep, lines trim in, numbers seat last) — the same law as the writing: nothing appears that wasn't computed.
Respect the reader: reduced-motion shows the final frame; the sky never exceeds its own layer; nothing moves while text is being read except the sky behind it.

P0Mechanics — the full contract
Where and when
Free tier only. Today and system screens. Fires once the screen settles: arrival cascade done + 6 s idle, scroll at rest. Never over the parchment, ceremony, journey, any sheet or paywall; never on People or Settings; never for subscribers.
Form and entrance
Emblem + «All of Alma», height 38, textured gold. Floats bottom-right: 16 from the edge, 12 above the bar. Enters with a 380 ms rise-and-fade; light shadow + gold halo so it reads above the sky.
Life — 11 seconds
Nods twice (a 4° wobble at 1 s and 6 s), then evaporates: 260 ms fade + 8 pt sink. Not modal — taps elsewhere never dismiss it, scroll slides it away with the content's inertia.
Caps
≤3 appearances per session · ≥90 s of silence between · resets daily. The static «Everything open, every day» line in the locked section is furniture and stays forever — the pill is the event that points at it.
Press — the morph
Light haptic. Scrim to 55 % in 240 ms; the sheet rises 380 ms. The emblem is the shared element — it travels from the pill into the sheet's seat while the pill body dissolves. Close reverses everything; Today underneath never reloads.
Memory
«Not now» on the sheet → the pill sleeps 7 days on that surface. Three dismissals in a row → it retires for this install; only the static line remains. Purchase → it never returns.
